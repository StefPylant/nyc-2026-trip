PLANNED
A southbound walk through old New York: St. Paul’s → Trinity Church → Wall Street → NYSE → Charging Bull → Battery Park.
👟 WALK: this entire stretch is the activity
🗺️ Map Area
Tap for the walking plan

1 · St. Paul’s Chapel

Already on the route from the WTC. This is the “Little Chapel That Stood,” which became a relief and rest site for rescue and recovery workers after 9/11.

2 · Trinity Church

Walk down Broadway to the historic church at Broadway and Wall Street. Alexander Hamilton is buried in the churchyard.

3 · Wall Street + Federal Hall + NYSE

Turn onto Wall Street. Look for Federal Hall, where George Washington took the oath of office as the first U.S. president, and the New York Stock Exchange across the street. This is a wander/photo stop, not a tour.

4 · Charging Bull

Continue south toward Bowling Green for the famous bronze bull. If the photo line is absurd, admire and keep moving.

5 · Battery Park

Finish at the southern tip of Manhattan for harbor views and a distant Statue of Liberty look. No ferry, no Liberty Island. The point is the view and the walk.

If you need a drink

O’Hara’s is the history-heavy option near the WTC. The Dead Rabbit is the high-priority cocktail option farther south and fits this route beautifully.
